ConsentGuard — Kitchen Sink

checking proxy…

A local playground for the consent-enforcing proxy. Grant analytics consent, fire a GA4 event, and watch the proxy either forward (via Measurement Protocol, if GA4_MEASUREMENT_ID and GA4_API_SECRET are set) or drop it. Other destinations are declared but not yet wired to real vendor APIs — they'll drop with a 204.

1. Consent

Your consent state is stored server-side, keyed to a cookie in this browser. No admin secret involved.

2. Fire events

These call real vendor endpoints. The client interceptor catches them and reroutes to the proxy.

"Stub" destinations have consent rules but no vendor adapter. They will drop with a 204 — the browser SDK sees a clean success.

3. What happened

Client-side log of every intercepted call. For the server-side view (with full audit trail and rule editor), open the admin dashboard.

The client bundle at /consentguard-client.js auto-initializes, patches fetch/XHR/beacon, and matches known tracker domains.
